Miners updates every 15 s

Works with AxeForge, AxeOS (Bitaxe) and NerdQAxe stock firmware. This miner polls the others itself, so their pages never have to be opened from here. Scanning checks every address in this miner's /24 network.

About "one pool per ASIC": all the chips share one serial chain and every job is broadcast to all of them (chips split the nonce range, the job packet carries no chip address). So pools can't be pinned to individual chips. Instead AxeForge splits the miner's work between pools by percentage — 4 pools at 25% each is the same hashrate as one chip per pool — or uses them as failover.

Mode

No reboot needed. Weights are normalized across enabled pools.

Solo payout check

Only for usernames that are a Bitcoin address (solo mining). With "don't send work", a pool whose block template would pay you less than 100% minus this fee gets no jobs until its next template does; the other pools carry on. 100 means any fee is fine and only a template that doesn't pay you at all is skipped.

Mode

Auto-tuneClimbs the clock, adds voltage on errors, stops at thermal/power/error walls, then trims voltage back down.

Per-chip clocksAfter the tuner locks, every chip that can go faster gets its own higher clock, in 12 MHz steps up to +48 MHz. Each chip is judged by its own error count; a chip that slips later is taken down a step on its own. Not used with the efficiency goal.

Changing the goal, the limit or per-chip clocks starts a new search.

Electricity price off

Stop mining when power is expensiveThe chips are switched off while the price is above your limit and start again once it drops (at most one change every 10 minutes). If the price can't be read, the miner keeps mining.

Manual point / tuner start

Used directly when auto-tune is off, and as the starting point when a new search begins.

Cooling

--
--

A header counts as connected once its fan has been seen spinning. Manual fans still go to 100% if a sensor is lost or the chips get within 3 °C of the ceiling.

Advanced chip timing

0 keeps the board's default. A longer job interval means fewer jobs and slightly more stale work; shorter means more UART traffic. Version-rolling speed exists on BM1370 boards only and is written to the chips right away.

Live screen --

This is the real LCD, captured from the miner every 2 s.

Pages & order

    Use the arrows to reorder. System stays on (it has the setup QR).

    Layout editor

    Custom pages need the 3.5" screen. On the T-Display-S3 they're skipped, but layouts you save here are kept for later.

    Drag to move, drag the corner to resize, click to select. 12 × 8 grid, same as the 480 × 272 page area. Fonts scale with widget size on the device.

    Screen settings

    Changing the screen reboots the miner. If the screen stays blank after switching, hold the bottom button while powering on to flip back.

    Rotate 180°Flip the screen if the miner is mounted upside down.

    Buttons on the miner: short press = next/previous page, hold top = backlight, hold bottom = restart auto-tune.

    Clock & weather

    24-hour clockTime from NTP; time zone detected from the city.
    FahrenheitUse °F and mph instead of °C and km/h.

    Weather by Open-Meteo, refreshed every 15 minutes. Only the city name and its coordinates are sent.

    Mempool feed

    Show mined & upcoming blocksRecent blocks, projected next blocks and fees, every 60 s.

    Use your own node's mempool instance here (e.g. http://umbrel.local:3006) to keep this private.

    Other connections

    Ethernet
    --

    A W5500 Ethernet adapter on the NerdQAxe header is found at boot. With a cable plugged in, the miner gets an address over the wire as well, and the setup Wi-Fi stays closed.

    Bluetooth setupWhile the setup network is open, the miner can also be set up with the free "ESP BLE Provisioning" app (it shows up as PROV_AXEFORGE_XXXX). Bluetooth turns off once Wi-Fi works.
    Accessory portLets add-ons made for AxeOS (displays, controllers) read and set the miner over the Bitaxe accessory header. Changing it reboots.

    Use the app image (axeforge.bin), not the factory image. Mining keeps running during the upload.

    Roll back a bad updateIf new firmware crashes at start-up twice, or is online but not mining 10 minutes after boot, the previous firmware starts again. It is kept once it has mined normally for 2 minutes.
